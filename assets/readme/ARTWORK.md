# README artwork

The cover is fictional decorative artwork made for this repository. It is not a source screenshot, a client document, or evidence for a claim mapping.

- **Cover:** [hero-handdrawn.png](hero-handdrawn.png)
- **Generation:** OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, September 7, 2026.
- **Direction:** hand-drawn pencil and ink, warm paper, soft watercolor accents.
- **Supporting graphics:** original SVG badges and line icons, authored directly for the README.
- **Use:** both the English and Chinese README share these local assets.

## Cover generation prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original panoramic GitHub README cover for Claim Chart, a patent-claim-to-evidence document workflow.
Primary request: a beautiful HAND-DRAWN illustration with the warmth and charm of a designer's illustrated research notebook. Entirely 2D pencil and fine black pen with flat, lightly textured watercolor washes, imperfect human linework, slightly wobbly hand-drawn borders. Warm cream paper background, generous whitespace. Fresh and sophisticated, playful but carefully composed.
Composition: 3:1 panoramic cover, approximately 1920 x 640. Large friendly hand-lettered title "Claim Chart" across the upper center, with a loose pale yellow marker underline. Along the bottom two thirds, a coherent left-to-right story: on the left a softly tilted patent sheet with small abstract claim lines and sage highlights; in the center a magnifying glass over a tiny technical mechanism drawing on a blue evidence note; on the right a finished two-column document with claim strokes neatly connected to colored evidence blocks and a small green check mark. Thin curved pencil arrows link the three illustrated objects. Small elegant notebook details: one binder clip, a pencil and a few restrained hand-drawn sparkles. Each subject should be large and clear, not a sea of tiny objects.
Color palette: warm off-white paper, charcoal ink, muted sage green, pale butter yellow, dusty sky blue, one tiny terracotta accent. No dark background.
Text: only the exact title "Claim Chart" and three small handwritten labels "CLAIMS", "EVIDENCE", "MAPPING". All other document text is abstract pencil strokes.
Avoid: 3D, isometric renders, photorealism, glossy glass, metallic gold, heavy drop shadows, gradients, rigid vector-perfect lines, corporate stock illustration, corporate logos, real company names, real patent numbers, watermarks, legal gavels or scales.
This is fictional decorative cover art, not a screenshot or source evidence.
```

The final cover was copied from the generated output without retouching. No real claim-chart material was used as input.
