# Research and Evidence Boundaries

Read when choosing the assessed product and evaluating sources, particularly when the named company uses a partner's technology or the sources describe older products.

## Establish the Assessed Product

Record in local work notes the target company, concrete product/service, supplier or operator, deployment/location, relevant period and patent identifier. Confirm the claim text and relevant dates from the patent itself. Keep this scope in the existing detailed Comment when the reference has no separate introduction.

Choose a product or service actually connected to the named company. A public offering using partner hardware can be a relevant assessed service, but it does not make the partner's entire technology portfolio the target's own system. If the user explicitly limits the task to technology developed or owned by the target, respect that restriction. Research the available connection before asking about an ambiguity that would change the comparison object.

## Build the Attribution Chain

For a partner feature, distinguish three questions:

1. What does the target's official material identify as supplied or deployed in this service?
2. What does the partner's technical material say about the named product or platform?
3. What connects that particular feature, version or operating mode to the assessed deployment?

A partnership announcement may answer the first question without answering the third. Keep the supplier's name attached to its technology. Do not infer ownership, manufacture, operation of a remote center or legal responsibility from a partnership alone.

Use plain evidence assessments in the existing prose:

- **Deployment confirmed:** an official source explicitly places the relevant feature in the assessed service/configuration.
- **Platform disclosed:** the supplier documents the feature, but the deployment's configuration is not established.
- **Correspondence inferred or unconfirmed:** the proposed functional match requires an assumption, claim interpretation or missing implementation evidence.

These are work-note distinctions, not a required new table or heading in the delivered chart. A concise summary must not turn a conditional detailed mapping into an unconditional match.

For example, a ride platform's announcement that a partner's automated driving system powers its service supports that system-level connection. A separate partner article about remote assistance does not by itself prove that remote assistance is enabled in the same service, or that its center performs the claimed traffic-control role.

## Time and Functional Limits

- Label old architecture descriptions as historical when the relevant configuration may have changed. Look for contemporary corroboration rather than silently carrying old hardware forward.
- After an acquisition or divestiture, verify continuity before attributing the former division's technology to the original company in a later period.
- Distinguish an announced plan, pilot, operator-attended deployment and driverless operation. A general safety requirement is not proof that a particular vehicle implements it.
- Match all functional parts of the limitation. Similar names or the presence of sensors plus connectivity do not establish a combined control path.
- For alternatives such as weather **and/or** special information, identify the supported alternative. Do not invent a weather feed or treat ordinary dispatch coordinates as hazard guidance.
- If the evidence cannot establish a limitation, say what remains unconfirmed. Identify the concrete missing evidence, such as a deployment specification, interface description or vehicle log, without fabricating a match or declaring an unsupported legal outcome.

## Keep Evidence Reviewable

Maintain a local source ledger with publisher, title, URL, publication/access date, supported claim rows, relevant passage/page, deployment connection, evidence strength and screenshot filename. Keep PDF printed-page numbers distinct from file-page numbers.

Capture actual source pages. Preserve enough surrounding context to identify the subject and qualification. Crop for readability, but do not stitch separated fragments into an apparently continuous sentence or recreate website text as a screenshot. Show separate excerpts and disclose omissions when necessary. Retain the original capture for review.

Use screenshots selectively and keep quoted text within applicable limits. Cite every source that materially supports a row, including sources used to establish the company/partner connection. If the template has no inline citation tags, put the source lines below the Comment. Keep source titles, dates, excerpt descriptions and page notes in the local ledger when the reference has no visible fields for them; a PDF page fragment may be included in the URL. Do not turn research metadata into additional document captions. Check that the top References list covers those sources in first-use order.

Source notes, client documents, evidence captures and render output remain local working materials. Do not add them to the public skill repository as regression fixtures; generate small synthetic fixtures instead.
